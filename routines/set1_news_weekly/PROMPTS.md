### Task text (verbatim)

```
TASK news — group the news articles found for {{subject.name}} into stories, and categorise each story.
Several publishers report the same event with different headlines; the database keeps ONE row per
story. You see headlines only; decide from the headlines, publishers and dates.

DOCUMENTS below hold two blocks of rows. The first block is NEW ARTICLES (handles C...; "owner" is
"company" or "product: <name>"; "copy_of" names an article the code already knows to be a copy of
this one). The second block is STORIES ALREADY STORED for {{subject.name}} in the last 45 days
(handles S...; "category" may be empty); "none on record" means there are none.

RULES
 - Group articles only when they report the SAME event: the same deal, contract, test, launch or
   announcement. A later follow-up event (the order, then its first delivery) is a different story.
   Same topic or same company on the same day is not enough.
 - Group by event, not by owner: one story may hold company articles and articles about one or more of
   the company's products when they report the same event. (The code writes every story to the
   company's news, and also to each product's news that has an article in it.)
 - Articles that share a "copy_of" are already known copies: put them in the same story.
 - Every C handle appears in exactly one story (a story may have a single article). Nothing is
   dropped: every article gets a story and a category.
 - same_as: a list of S handles of already stored stories that report the same event (the event can be
   stored under the company and under a product, so there may be several); [] when it is new.
 - category: exactly one of
     Contract & order         an order, contract, tender win or selection to supply defence goods or services
     Partnership / JV         a partnership, MoU, teaming, joint venture, licence or technology tie-up
     Product launch & testing a product unveiled, tested, trialled or certified
     Delivery & induction     a delivery, handover or induction into service
     Export                   a sale, approval or contract with a foreign customer
     Financial results        reported quarterly/annual results, profit, revenue, order book, guidance
     Stock & market           share-price moves, analyst or broker notes, fund holdings, "breakout" or volume
                              lists, stock tips
     Facility & capacity      a new plant, expansion, investment, jobs, production capacity
     Leadership & corporate   management changes, strategy statements, restructuring, acquisitions, accidents
     Non-defence business     the company's civil, infrastructure, energy or EPC business with no defence
                              angle (metro, offshore platform, chemical plant, real-estate corridor): a civil
                              contract is Non-defence business, not Contract & order
     Other                    none of the above
   Financial results is what the company reported or guided; Stock & market is how the share or the
   market reacts to it. Give a category for every story, also when same_as is set.
 - JUDGEMENT fields (each needs "_reason"): category (one sentence); candidates, only when the story
   groups 2 or more articles (one sentence: why they are the same event).

OUTPUT: records of table "news_story", one per story. "candidates" is ALWAYS the list of C handles of
the story, also for a story of a single article (never null or empty); every C handle must be listed:
  {"table": "news_story",
   "values": {"candidates": ["C1a2b3c4d", ...], "same_as": ["S5e6f7a8b"] or [], "category": "...",
              "_reason": {"category": "...", "candidates": "..."}}}
skip_reason only when there are no new articles.

CLIENT: {{context.client}}
COMPETITORS:
{{context.competitors}}

DOCUMENTS (new articles, then stored stories):
{{documents}}
```

   {{client.name}} product(s) from CLIENT PRODUCTS it touches, if any

 - source_name: the publisher; source_url: the article URL
 competitive_signal_evidence (3-8 rows):
 - evidence_group: "fact" (a figure or named detail stated in the article: quantity, value,

   Allowed: fact | key_point

URL: {{subject.article_url}}

STEP 1 — RELEVANCE. It is relevant only if both hold:
 a) it reports a TECHNOLOGY DEVELOPMENT: a new technology, material or process, an R&D result, a

 - article_says: neutral summary, 3-6 sentences, max 900 characters, plain text, only what the

   recommend an action
 - classification: JUDGEMENT. competitor_advance if the developer is one of COMPETITORS (match
   name or short name); adoptable if the developer is anyone else and {{client.name}} could adopt
   or integrate the technology. Allowed: competitor_advance | adoptable
 - direction: JUDGEMENT. threat if the development strengthens a competitor against
   {{client.name}} in a category where {{client.name}} has a product; fav if it is favourable to
   {{client.name}} (adoptable technology for its products, or a setback for a competitor);
   watch otherwise (announced, planned, early-stage, or claimed without a demonstrated result).
   Allowed: threat | watch | fav
 - article_date: the date of the development as the article states it; else the published date
   (say so in _precision)

 children:
 technology_overview_fact (2-5 rows, "at a glance"):

 - fact_value; proof_text: the verbatim article sentence that names the organisation and its role
   (max 300 characters)

 - point_text: one key point, one sentence, max 200 characters, a reported fact (what it is, what was
   shown, figures stated), not an opinion
 - evidence_text: the verbatim article sentence it rests on (max 300 characters)

 - display_label: the name as the article writes it; ref_competitor: the COMPETITORS name if the
   same company, else null

 technology_overview_source (exactly one row, the article):
 - source_url, publisher, source_kind (news | official | company); is_primary true

CLIENT: {{context.client}}
