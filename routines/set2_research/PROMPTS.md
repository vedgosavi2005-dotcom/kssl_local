{{subject.display_name}} itself or one of its own units or subsidiaries, and never {{client.name}}.

PARTNER NAMES. {{client.name}}'s dashboard detects a partner shared with {{client.name}} by exact
name. If the organisation (same legal entity or same named unit, also under an alias) is in the

unit is the party in the source. Never shorten to an acronym unless the organisation does.

 - partner_name: as above

   supply of products, subsystems or services, either way), Technology partner (licence,
   transfer of technology, co-development, integration), MoU / strategic (MoU, teaming,
   strategic agreement), Acquisition (one acquired the other or a unit of it), Other. If several
   ties exist, take the first that applies in that order.
